James (JD) Longmire is a Senior Technical Fellow in the aerospace and defense sector and an independent researcher. He is a working enterprise architect, and he writes Thinx-Tank from inside the work: IT operations, the digital enterprise, and applied AI. His focus is governance: who decides, who is accountable, and what happens when autonomous systems outrun the processes built to control them.

His current work includes AIOC, the AI Operations Center architecture, a governed control plane for the enterprise AI ecosystem, and MxM, his multi-mode governance frame for human-directed AI systems. He works under the operating principle Human-Curated, AI-Enabled (HCAE): AI proposes, a named human disposes.

A portfolio of his public and private work is on the [Portfolio page](https://blog.thinxai.net/portfolio/). He can be reached at jdlongmire@outlook.com.
