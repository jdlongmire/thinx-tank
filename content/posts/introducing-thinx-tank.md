This is Thinx-Tank: my notebook on IT, the digital enterprise, and applied AI. This post is the tour. Short version: I write about making technology actually work inside real organizations, from someone who's spent a career doing exactly that.

## The name

It's a deliberate pun. A think tank studies and advises. A **Thinx** tank is where ideas about intelligent systems get worked, tested, and put into service. The emphasis is on the second half of that sentence. Thinking is cheap; the tank is where things get built, break, and get fixed.

## Who's writing

I'm a practitioner, not a pundit. I've spent my career inside large enterprises doing the unglamorous work of making technology serve the business: architecture, integration, operations, and now the messy, interesting business of putting AI to work where it counts. I've sat in the rooms where build-vs-buy gets decided, where an outage becomes a postmortem, where a promising pilot dies on contact with procurement. That perspective is the whole point of this blog.

## What you'll find here

Three threads, braided together:

- **IT and operations:** the plumbing. Infrastructure, integration, reliability, and the unsexy work that keeps the lights on.
- **The digital enterprise:** architecture, strategy, and governance. How large organizations decide what to build, what to buy, and what to kill.
- **Applied AI:** not the hype cycle, the deployment cycle. Agentic systems in production, AI governance that survives contact with auditors, and honest field notes on what worked and what didn't.

Expect posts on enterprise architecture, AI strategy and governance, the integration work that makes AI deployments real, and occasional notes from the lab. I'm usually building something, and the interesting failures end up here.

## The house stance

Signal over hype. If a technology can't survive contact with a production environment and a budget line, it doesn't belong here. I don't do vendor cheerleading, and I don't do doom. I do trade-offs, evidence, and the occasional strong opinion earned the hard way.

One more thing: every post here ships with a visual: a hero graphic or an infographic. Dense topics deserve more than a wall of text, and most of you will read this on a phone.

## What's coming

First up: the agentic execution control plane: why AI agents should never be trusted production principals, and the architecture that keeps them useful without letting them delete your database. After that, notes on AI governance, the digital thread, and whatever breaks next in the lab.

Welcome aboard. The tank is open.
