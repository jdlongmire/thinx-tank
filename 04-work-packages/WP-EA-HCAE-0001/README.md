# WP-EA-HCAE-0001 — Human-Curated, AI-Enabled Enterprise Architecture

Status: active. Repository: jdlongmire/thinx-tank.
Owner: JD Longmire.

## Purpose

Develop a concise mentoring package that teaches a developing architect what Enterprise Architecture (EA) is, why it matters, and how Generative AI can increase an architect's reach without displacing human architectural authority.

The primary deliverable is a ten-minute spoken narrative suitable for a protégé. Supporting artifacts will make the model reusable for mentoring, executive communication, and later Thinx-Tank publication.

## Core proposition

> Enterprise Architecture is the discipline of maintaining intentional coherence as an enterprise changes. Generative AI can dramatically increase the architect's reach when the capability remains human-curated and AI-enabled.

## Architecture model

The narrative uses this value chain:

**Strategy → Architecture → Roadmaps → Standards → Delivery**

The architecture practice closes the loop through:

**Govern → Assure → Adapt → Measure**

GenAI operates horizontally across the value chain. Human judgment, provenance, governance, and decision authority form the control plane.

## HCAE principle

Human-Curated, AI-Enabled (HCAE) means humans continuously establish and curate authoritative sources, semantics, intent, policy, acceptance criteria, and consequential decisions. AI supplies high-volume synthesis, correlation, generation, exploration, traceability support, and checking.

Human curation is therefore an active lifecycle function, not a final approval gate on machine-generated work.

## Learning objectives

After the ten-minute discussion, the protégé should be able to:

1. Explain EA as an enterprise coherence discipline rather than a diagram-production function.
2. Trace strategic intent through architecture, roadmaps, standards, and delivery.
3. Distinguish architectural judgment from the information-processing work surrounding it.
4. Identify GenAI tasks that can safely increase an architect's leverage.
5. Explain why authoritative knowledge, provenance, human disposition, and governance are prerequisites for trustworthy AI-enabled EA.
6. Recognize the emerging architect role as curator and steward of a living enterprise knowledge system.

## Acceptance criteria

The package is complete when:

- the narrative can be delivered conversationally in approximately ten minutes;
- EA is presented as an operating discipline connecting strategy to implementation;
- AI augmentation is concrete rather than promotional;
- human and AI responsibilities are explicitly separated;
- HCAE provenance, verification, disposition, and accountability are preserved;
- the material can be reused without requiring proprietary enterprise details; and
- JD has reviewed and accepted the narrative and visual model.
