# WP-MXM-INDUSTRY-0001 — MxM Industry Contribution

Status: active

## Purpose

Determine, with documented prior-art research and falsifiable comparison, whether MxM contributes a distinct and useful architecture to the broader AI-agent corpus. The work must distinguish originality, utility, and demonstrated external contribution rather than assuming novelty.

## Research question

Can a model- and harness-neutral meta-harness separate probabilistic AI cognition from persistent identity, governance, memory, procedure, capability, evidence, and human acceptance sufficiently to preserve those invariants across heterogeneous agent runtimes?

## Authority and artifact boundaries

- Canonical MxM architecture remains upstream in `ologos-repos/thinx`.
- Reference implementation candidates, including `mxm-kernel`, are evidence-bearing implementations rather than the source of architectural authority.
- This work package owns comparative research, analysis, experimental framing, and publication development.
- Final scholarly papers graduate to `papers/mxm/`.
- Public exposition for blog.thinxai.net graduates to `content/posts/`.

## Workstreams

### 1. Prior art
Research primary sources first. Compare MxM with relevant agent architecture, governance, safety, orchestration, memory, tool/capability, evidence/tracing, and human-approval constructs. Record sources and confidence. Do not claim novelty from terminology alone.

### 2. Comparative analysis
Identify which MxM elements are already satisfied by prior art, which are compositions of known elements, and which may constitute distinct architectural contributions. Classify findings as ADOPT, ADAPT, ALREADY SATISFIED, REJECT, or DEFER where a disposition is required.

### 3. Novelty claims
State narrowly testable claims. A candidate contribution must identify an architectural property or invariant, not merely a renamed concept.

### 4. Hypotheses
Initial hypotheses:

- H1: MxM can preserve governance invariants across heterogeneous model providers.
- H2: Means implementations can be replaced without altering Mission, Morals, or Mind semantics.
- H3: Kernel-mediated Evidence can distinguish model claims about execution from demonstrable execution.
- H4: Operator Acceptance can remain invariant across autonomous agent workflows.
- H5: Explicit Mind/Methods separation can reduce epistemic-policy drift across models.

These are hypotheses, not findings. Revise or reject them against evidence.

### 5. Reference-kernel evaluation
Evaluate the supplied MxM kernel against canonical MxM. Build specification-to-conformance traceability. Treat implementation behavior as evidence, not as definition of the architecture.

### 6. Publication
Produce:
1. a scholarly paper suitable for external archival/publication;
2. a concise technical article for blog.thinxai.net;
3. supporting figures and comparison artifacts;
4. a reproducible source ledger.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-01: Primary-source landscape research is documented; inaccessible primary sources are identified explicitly.
- AC-02: Existing industry concepts are not represented as MxM inventions.
- AC-03: Candidate MxM contributions are stated as precise architectural claims.
- AC-04: Claims have supporting evidence, counterarguments, limitations, and confidence.
- AC-05: Hypotheses have defined evaluation criteria and potential falsifiers.
- AC-06: MxM is compared at the appropriate abstraction level; frameworks with different purposes are not falsely presented as direct competitors.
- AC-07: Reference-kernel claims map to executable evidence and conformance tests.
- AC-08: The paper distinguishes conceptual novelty, demonstrated utility, and external adoption/validation.
- AC-09: Scholarly output has stable author/version/citation metadata and a complete source ledger.
- AC-10: Blog treatment accurately represents the scholarly result without overstating it.
