# Comparative Analysis

This artifact will compare MxM against adjacent industry and research constructs at their appropriate abstraction levels.

## Required distinctions

For each comparator identify:

1. purpose and abstraction level;
2. concepts overlapping MxM;
3. concepts absent or differently scoped;
4. whether MxM merely renames, composes, extends, or introduces a distinct property;
5. evidence for the assessment;
6. limitations and counterarguments.

No overall ranking is required. The objective is architectural discrimination.
