# Candidate Novelty Claims

Status: hypotheses pending research.

Candidate areas to test:

1. Six-surface model/harness-neutral decomposition: Mind, Morals, Mission, Memory, Methods, Means.
2. Explicit architectural separation between probabilistic cognition and deterministic governance/execution.
3. Evidence and human Acceptance as first-class architectural concepts distinct from model self-report.
4. Explicit Mind/Methods separation, including inference type -> warrant -> confidence as normative epistemic posture while procedures remain separately governable.
5. Preservation of identity/governance/memory invariants across replaceable models, harnesses, and Means implementations.

Terminology, alliteration, and category naming are not themselves novelty claims.
