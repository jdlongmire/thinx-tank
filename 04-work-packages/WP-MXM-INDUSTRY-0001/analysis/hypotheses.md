# Falsifiable Hypotheses

## H1 — Governance portability
MxM can preserve specified governance invariants across heterogeneous model providers.

Potential falsifier: a required invariant necessarily depends on provider-specific model behavior rather than the meta-harness.

## H2 — Means substitutability
A Means implementation can be replaced without changing Mission, Morals, or Mind semantics.

Potential falsifier: replacing a capability implementation requires redefining one of those semantic surfaces rather than only its binding or policy.

## H3 — Evidence discrimination
Kernel-mediated Evidence can reliably distinguish a model's assertion that an action occurred from demonstrable execution.

Potential falsifier: consequential execution cannot be independently evidenced at the governance boundary.

## H4 — Acceptance invariance
Human Acceptance can remain an explicit authority transition across autonomous workflows.

Potential falsifier: a target workflow requires equating successful autonomous execution with Acceptance.

## H5 — Mind/Methods separation
Separating normative epistemic posture from procedural Methods can reduce cross-model epistemic-policy drift.

Potential falsifier: the separation produces no measurable preservation advantage, or cannot be operationalized without collapsing Mind into Methods.
