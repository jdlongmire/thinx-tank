# MxM: A Model- and Harness-Neutral Meta-Architecture for Governed AI Agents

**Author:** James D. (JD) Longmire  
**ORCID:** 0009-0009-1383-7698  
**Status:** Working draft  
**Version:** 0.1

## Abstract

To be developed from the evidence produced by WP-MXM-INDUSTRY-0001. The paper will test whether MxM provides a defensible architectural contribution to governed AI-agent systems rather than presupposing that conclusion.

## Research question

Can a model- and harness-neutral meta-harness separate probabilistic AI cognition from persistent identity, governance, memory, procedure, capability, evidence, and human acceptance sufficiently to preserve those invariants across heterogeneous agent runtimes?
